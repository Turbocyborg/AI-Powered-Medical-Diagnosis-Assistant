HEART DISEASE PREDICTION - FEATURE MAPPING
==========================================

Dataset: Cardiovascular Disease Dataset
Features: 12
Target: Heart Disease (0=No, 1=Yes)

FEATURES:

1. age (int)
   - Age in years
   - Range: 20-80

2. gender (binary)
   - 0 = Female
   - 1 = Male

3. chestpain (categorical: 0-3)
   - 0 = Typical angina
   - 1 = Atypical angina
   - 2 = Non-anginal pain
   - 3 = Asymptomatic

4. restingBP (int)
   - Resting blood pressure (mm Hg)
   - Range: 94-200

5. serumcholestrol (int)
   - Serum cholesterol (mg/dl)
   - Range: 126-564

6. fastingbloodsugar (binary)
   - Fasting blood sugar > 120 mg/dl
   - 0 = No (≤120 mg/dl)
   - 1 = Yes (>120 mg/dl)

7. restingrelectro (categorical: 0-2)
   - Resting electrocardiographic results
   - 0 = Normal
   - 1 = ST-T wave abnormality
   - 2 = Left ventricular hypertrophy

8. maxheartrate (int)
   - Maximum heart rate achieved
   - Range: 71-202

9. exerciseangia (binary)
   - Exercise induced angina
   - 0 = No
   - 1 = Yes

10. oldpeak (float)
    - ST depression induced by exercise
    - Range: 0.0-6.2

11. slope (categorical: 0-3)
    - Slope of peak exercise ST segment
    - 0 = Downsloping
    - 1 = Flat
    - 2 = Upsloping
    - 3 = Unknown

12. noofmajorvessels (categorical: 0-3)
    - Number of major vessels colored by fluoroscopy
    - Range: 0-3

TARGET:
- 0 = No Heart Disease
- 1 = Heart Disease Present

DATASET STATISTICS:
- Total samples: 1000
- Heart Disease: 580 (58%)
- No Disease: 420 (42%)
- Missing values: 0
- All features are numeric

MODEL PERFORMANCE:
- Best Model: Gradient Boosting
- Accuracy: 98.5%
- AUC Score: 0.9994
- Very high accuracy due to strong correlations
