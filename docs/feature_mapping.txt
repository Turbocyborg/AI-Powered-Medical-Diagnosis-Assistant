
BRFSS DIABETES DATASET - FEATURE MAPPING GUIDE
==============================================

INPUT FEATURES (21 total):

1. DEMOGRAPHIC FEATURES (4):
   - Age: 1-13 (age groups from 18-24 to 80+)
   - Sex: 0=Female, 1=Male
   - Education: 1-6 (Never attended to College graduate)
   - Income: 1-8 ($10K increments from <$10K to $75K+)

2. HEALTH CONDITIONS (6):
   - HighBP: 0=No, 1=Yes (High Blood Pressure)
   - HighChol: 0=No, 1=Yes (High Cholesterol)
   - CholCheck: 0=No, 1=Yes (Cholesterol check in 5 years)
   - BMI: Continuous (Body Mass Index, typically 15-50)
   - Stroke: 0=No, 1=Yes (Ever had a stroke)
   - HeartDiseaseorAttack: 0=No, 1=Yes (Heart disease/attack)

3. LIFESTYLE FACTORS (5):
   - Smoker: 0=No, 1=Yes (Smoked 100+ cigarettes)
   - PhysActivity: 0=No, 1=Yes (Physical activity in 30 days)
   - Fruits: 0=No, 1=Yes (Consume fruit 1+ times/day)
   - Veggies: 0=No, 1=Yes (Consume vegetables 1+ times/day)
   - HvyAlcoholConsump: 0=No, 1=Yes (Heavy alcohol consumption)

4. HEALTHCARE ACCESS (6):
   - AnyHealthcare: 0=No, 1=Yes (Have health coverage)
   - NoDocbcCost: 0=No, 1=Yes (Couldn't see doctor due to cost)
   - GenHlth: 1-5 (1=Excellent to 5=Poor general health)
   - MentHlth: 0-30 (Days of poor mental health)
   - PhysHlth: 0-30 (Days of poor physical health)
   - DiffWalk: 0=No, 1=Yes (Difficulty walking/climbing stairs)

TARGET VARIABLE:
   - Diabetes_binary: 0=No Diabetes, 1=Prediabetes/Diabetes

DATASET STATISTICS:
   - Total samples: ~253,680 (BRFSS 2015)
   - Binary features: 14
   - Continuous features: 3  
   - Ordinal features: 4
   - Typical class distribution: ~85% No Diabetes, ~15% Diabetes
