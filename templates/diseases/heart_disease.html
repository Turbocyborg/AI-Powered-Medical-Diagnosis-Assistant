About Heart Disease Prediction

This AI model uses machine learning to assess cardiovascular disease risk based on clinical measurements and patient history. The model analyzes 12 clinical indicators including:

  • Demographics: Age and gender factors
  • Symptoms: Chest pain type and exercise-induced angina
  • Vital Signs: Blood pressure, cholesterol, and heart rate measurements
  • Cardiac Tests: ECG results, ST depression, and fluoroscopy findings

Note: This tool is trained on cardiovascular disease datasets and provides risk assessment for educational purposes. Always consult with a healthcare professional for proper medical diagnosis.
