About Liver Disease Prediction

This AI model uses machine learning to assess liver disease risk based on liver function test results. The model analyzes 10 clinical indicators including:

  • Bilirubin Levels: Indicators of liver's ability to process waste
  • Liver Enzymes: SGPT/ALT, SGOT/AST, and Alkaline Phosphatase levels
  • Protein Levels: Total proteins, albumin, and A/G ratio
  • Demographics: Age and gender factors

Note: This tool is trained on the Indian Liver Patient dataset and provides risk assessment for educational purposes.
