About Pneumonia Detection

This AI model uses deep learning (Convolutional Neural Network) to detect pneumonia from chest X-ray images. The model analyzes visual patterns in the X-ray to identify signs of pneumonia infection.

  • Model Architecture: ResNet18 with transfer learning (pretrained on ImageNet)
  • Training Dataset: Chest X-Ray Images (Pneumonia) dataset with thousands of labeled X-rays
  • Performance: 86.54% accuracy, 99.49% sensitivity (recall), 0.96 AUC-ROC
  • Image Requirements: Clear chest X-ray images in JPG, JPEG, or PNG format
Note: This model is trained specifically on chest X-ray images. For best results, upload a clear, front-facing chest X-ray. The model cannot diagnose other conditions or analyze other types of medical images.
